FAQ

Frequently asked questions

These entries are enough to validate the FAQ section and typography.

Can I use this theme for a real product?

Yes. The example site is intentionally minimal, but the theme is designed for production Hugo sites.

Can I translate homepage content?

Yes. Add language-specific content files such as _index.zh.md and translated legal pages.

Do I need analytics or social links?

No. Those blocks render only when the consuming site provides values.
